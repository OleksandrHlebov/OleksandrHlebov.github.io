Unreal Engine 5 C++ Team of 6

Worked in a team of 6 people developing in Unreal Engine 5. Assisted artists in development and integration of their assets, and developed a small in-engine tool for artists to easily distribute valuable spawns on the map. Did extensive debugging and troubleshooting of own and colleagues' code, using Perforce as version control.

Main gameplay mechanics worked on:

  • Throwing objects and their interactions with the world
  • Collection zones for valuable items
  • Fragile objects using the UE5 Chaos destruction system, including C++ triggers with force fields
  • Custom physics override for character interaction with dynamic objects
  • Animation programming including animation blueprints, blending animations, scripted animated sequences, animation montages, and C++ multicast-delegate based triggers of animations
